06. Dates and logistics to check

Adventures Wheel

  • Trip duration: 6 days / 5 nights.
  • Official page advises arriving in Guwahati by 9:00 AM on Day 1.
  • Book onward departure after 7:00 PM on Day 6.
  • Bike rider package requires a refundable ₹10,000 security deposit per bike.
  • Published group departure age range: 16–42 years.

Ekkarvaan

  • Published November batch: 23–28 November 2026.
  • Other published November batches include 16–21 and 30 Nov–5 Dec.
  • Booking amount: ₹5,000 per seat.
  • Balance payable 7 days before departure or on departure day.
  • Page states booking is non-refundable.
Date mismatch to resolve: The earlier plan discussed 21–28 November, but Ekkarvaan's listed batch is 23–28 November (6 days). If you travel from Goa on 21 November, ask whether Ekkarvaan can accommodate the dates or budget separately for the extra nights. Adventures Wheel's current page does not expose a confirmed departure date in the text reviewed—confirm the exact batch.

07. Questions to ask before paying

Use these questions to get comparable written answers from both operators.

01 · Confirm final priceGet the final per-person price including GST, room sharing, transport type and any seasonal surcharge.
02 · Confirm exact datesConfirm pickup time, drop time, arrival deadline and whether extra nights are required.
03 · Double Decker trekAsk whether a local guide, entry charges and the full trek logistics are included; confirm expected walking time.
04 · Dawki boatingAsk Ekkarvaan for the current boat price per person and whether it is shared or private. Confirm AW's included boating duration/activity.
05 · Transport & group sizeConfirm Tempo Traveller vs SUV, seat capacity, luggage space and expected group size.
06 · AccommodationAsk for the stay names / standard, especially AW's alpine camp at Shnongpdeng and Ekkarvaan's Dawki stay.
07 · MealsConfirm which breakfasts/dinner are included and whether vegetarian options are available.
08 · Cancellation & safetyRead the cancellation policy, weather contingency, medical support and refund terms before paying.

08. Our shortlist recommendation

Best fit for no-bike + included activity

Adventures Wheel Tempo

Strong choice if you want the Shnongpdeng camp experience, boating included, the guided root-bridge trek and a trip aligned to the original schedule—provided the quoted Tempo price and departure are reconfirmed.

Best fit for broader sightseeing mix

Ekkarvaan

Attractive if Mawphlang, Seven Sisters, Krang Suri and the Jowai route are priorities. Confirm the packed Day 5 schedule, Dawki boat charges, trek guide and the 23–28 November batch dates.

Bottom line: Don't decide on the headline package price alone. The non-bike package rates are nearly identical; compare the actual transport, accommodation, trek support, Dawki activity, dates and written final quote.
